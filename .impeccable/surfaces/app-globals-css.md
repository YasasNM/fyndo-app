## Direction contract
THESIS: Every place reads like a stop on a Galle Road bus board: its area on a plate, its price as the route number. Refuses the white delivery-app grid with one green accent.

OWN-WORLD: Bus-livery red shell (#B8202B top band, active nav) over a cool board-white ground (#F2F2EE), enamel-white cards with a thin rule. Route-board yellow (#FFD000) on board black (#141414) only for area plates, the destination-blind search and the one primary action. Green (#13804A) confirms and marks open; chili marks closed and errors. Saira Condensed for plates, names and numerals; Atkinson Hyperlegible for reading. Dark: board-black ground, darker red shell, yellow plates unchanged.

STORY: Someone undecided sees what's open, where it is and what it really costs, narrows by mood, opens a place, then plans, saves or books.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone: red band with pin wordmark; black destination-blind search with yellow placeholder; mood plates; "Open near you" photo cards: yellow area plate top-left, open dot fixed top-right, name left, per-person price huge right; bottom nav, red active marker. Desktop: nav inside the red band, hero video beside the search.

FORM: Galle Road bus board, candidate 3 of 7 grounded, seed:b7411164e23cb83e. Signature move: area plates and prices set as route-board numerals; the planner drawn as a route strip of numbered stops. Raises: hierarchy by scale alone (specimen); nothing labelled twice (catalog sleeve); the night fits its time and budget (j-card); state as a mark in a fixed cell (timetable).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
